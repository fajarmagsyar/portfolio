## Album covers — copyrighted, not licensed

The two posters (one on the wall, one in a standing frame) show the covers of *OK Computer* (Radiohead, 1997) and *DAMN.* (Kendrick Lamar, 2017), from `public/art/`. The artwork belongs to the artists and their labels and is shown here as a fan's posters, without a licence. The layout around each cover (title, artist, year, track list) was made for this site.
